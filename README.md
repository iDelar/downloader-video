# Clipium — Telegram Video Downloader Bot

Free Telegram bot to download videos from **TikTok**, **Instagram Reels**, **YouTube Shorts**, and **Pinterest** — without watermarks, in original quality.

🌐 **Website:** [idelar.github.io/downloader-video](https://idelar.github.io/downloader-video/)  
🤖 **Telegram bot:** [@clipiumbot](https://t.me/clipiumbot?start=GitHub)

## Features

- 🎵 Download TikTok videos without watermark
- 📸 Save Instagram Reels, posts, and stories
- ▶️ Download YouTube Shorts and regular videos
- 📌 Grab Pinterest videos and pins
- 🌍 Available in 16 languages
- 📱 Works on iPhone, Android, Windows, Mac, Linux
- 💯 100% free, no ads, no subscription

## How to Use

1. Open [@clipiumbot](https://t.me/clipiumbot?start=GitHub) in Telegram
2. Send a link to a video from TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, or Pinterest
3. Get your video file in seconds — no watermark, full quality
